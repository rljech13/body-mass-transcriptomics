# Boundaries of the saved results

- Pathway membership refinement selected genes associated with mass in the parent sum's direction. Selection and testing used the same data. The nine-selected-group BH family does not account for the preceding selection.
- The epiblast OXPHOS result is conditional on the stated assignment/filter. A different neighboring analysis gave a different outcome; this repository does not resolve that conflict by removing one version from the scientific record.
- The splicing panel measures expression of14genes, not alternative-splicing PSI. The ordinary association and the saved phylogenetic q=0.36 are separate results.
- The displayed WGCNA panel is a descriptive Pearson/BH analysis of41frozen non-grey modules. Network inference and historical PGLS results are retained separately.
- pySCENIC used primary seed20261023 and sensitivity seeds20261024/20261025. Repeated-run significance varied. ERG/FOXP4 target counts refer to the primary displayed regulons, not proven binding targets.
- Promoter motif matches nominate shared regulators of gene groups. They do not establish TF activity, occupancy, metabolic flux or causal size regulation.
- Current helper files not matched to an executed historical snapshot are labeled as recovered helper sources in the provenance manifest. Their presence is not proof of execution.
